Assumptions that always apply

  • US commercial primary airports; New England = CT, ME, MA, NH, RI, VT
  • Profit proxy is capacity unlock, not passenger facility charge, bond, or NPV
  • T-100 is segment traffic, not true origin-and-destination itineraries
  • Long-haul default 4000 km (Eurocontrol) on T-100 segments
  • Live FAA status is operations delay, not passenger demand
  • Missing gates, TAF, or NPIAS weights are dropped and disclosed
